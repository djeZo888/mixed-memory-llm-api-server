The local defect is confirmed. On ai-vm, Docker29.6.1 / containerd2.2.5 /
runc1.3.6, systemd255 (255.4-1ubuntu8.17), systemd cgroup-v2:
a disposable64MiB no-GPU container changed its raw `memory.swap.max` from `0`
to `max` immediately after `systemctl daemon-reload`. Its `memory.max` remained
67108864, swap use0 and OOM0. This confirms the local mechanism described in
[Moby51446](https://github.com/moby/moby/issues/51446); H017's missing failing

policy states and verified the sole exact child. No large allocation, model
mount, GPU device, network, inference, download or native build was involved.

prototype slice files were removed. No Docker/systemd daemon or host restart
