## Implemented and verified

- Independent read-only [status](http://10.156.100.61/status) and typed
  [administration](http://10.156.100.61/admin), within the approved trusted LAN.
- Services bind to GPU UUIDs. Missing/faulted hardware isolates its dependent
  service; unknown telemetry is not treated as missing hardware. Same-boot
  hardware latches cannot be cleared by an ordinary service restart.
- Native schedulers busy-poll for 600 seconds after real work completes, then
  block while retaining model allocations. Passive status polling does not
  extend the grace period. New work wakes the scheduler.
- Scoped ownership, interruption handling and durable operation records prevent
  blind replay of uncertain work. Private failure diagnostics now distinguish
  relay failure from upstream rejection without recording credentials or raw
  response bodies.
