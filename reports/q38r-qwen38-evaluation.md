## Capacity plan: actual total tokens, one sequence

Established hardware: two 97,887 MiB GPUs (**95.593 GiB each**, 191.19 aggregate), SM120, no NVLink; 881.78 GiB RAM, Threadripper 9985WX. Claimed eight-channel ~400 GB/s RAM bandwidth is unmeasured. VRAM pools are separate.

**BF16 KV** = `tokens × 16 layers × 2(K,V) × 4 KV heads × 256 × 2 bytes` = **65,536 bytes/token**. FP8 weights do not imply FP8 KV. One linear-state slot = `48 × [(48×128×128×4) + ((2×16×128+48×128)×3×2)]` = **153,944,064 bytes**: FP32 recurrent state, BF16 convolution. TP2 divides both evenly. Release pool allocates `size+1`; a capped one-slot pool therefore costs **0.286743 GiB aggregate**, including its sentinel. [State geometry/dtypes](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/0bcd822377da7b5718e674eaf9c870d349424dd1/python/sglang/srt/configs/mamba_utils.py), [pool](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/0bcd822377da7b5718e674eaf9c870d349424dd1/python/sglang/srt/mem_cache/memory_pool.py).

Planning budgets below include **8 GiB workspace + 8 GiB safety reserve per GPU**. Weight allowance: 32 GiB TP1, 18 GiB/GPU TP2; these exceed serialized 28.747/ideal-half 14.374 GiB, allowing padding/layout/replication. Even duplicating the entire outside shard gives a 17.171 GiB/GPU weight proxy. These allowances are assumptions, not measured upper bounds.

Bounds require one running request, `--max-mamba-cache-size 1`, disabled radix cache/speculation/ReplaySSM/graphs, BF16 KV and `--max-total-tokens` equal to the context cap. Otherwise preallocated pools can exceed this arithmetic. Budget includes all serialized vision/MTP weights conservatively; excludes additional sequences, retained prefix states, multimodal activations, speculative buffers and transient load copies. CUDA contexts, allocator fragmentation, attention/collective buffers and chunked-prefill activations must fit the assumed workspace/reserve; verify actual peaks. Disabling radix means repeated tool turns can re-prefill history and is an initial acceptance setting.

**Optional extension only:** 1,000,000 tokens needs 61.035 GiB BF16 KV: TP1 plan **109.32 GiB (NO-GO)**; TP2 **64.66/GPU, 129.32 aggregate (capacity arithmetic only)**. Native config has no extension override; approved RoPE/YaRN configuration, quality and latency validation are missing. Do not substitute 1,048,576 for the card's 1,000,000 or launch at an advertised maximum.

## Comparison and one recommended future path

Coder-Next FP8 is 80B/3B-active MoE, 74.861 GiB weights, non-thinking and coding-focused. Its 12 full-attention layers × 2 KV heads × 256 BF16 cache need **3/6 GiB at 128K/256K**; Qwen3.8 needs **2.67× more KV** despite smaller weights. Dense 27B compute versus ~3B-active MoE does **not** establish faster prefill/decode; thinking adds tokens. Single GPU avoids PCIe collectives; TP2 offers more headroom with communication costs. All-GPU fit proves no throughput advantage. [Pinned comparator config/card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Coder-Next-FP8/tree/da6e2ed27304dd39abadd9c82ef50e8de67bdd4c).

Official quality scores are not a common deployment benchmark: Qwen3.8 SWE-bench Pro uses Claude Code, temperature 1/top-p .95 and 256K, with corrected tasks/re-evaluated baselines; QwenSWEBench is in-house avg@3, eight-hour timeout/32,768 output. Its FP8 near-parity claim lacks our hardware validation; Coder-Next explicitly reports original BF16 results. Infer neither tool-quality ranking nor FP8 speed from those tables. [Qwen3.8 card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8/blob/017b9c7af6b5689d5dd426a76e0bc077eb5ca20a/README.md).
